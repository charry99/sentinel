Sentinel — public track record

An autonomous agent that turns a free public signal into a live prediction market on Solana devnet (pm-AMM), resolves it from the public source named in the market's criteria, and publishes the proof — win or lose. No real money: devnet + mock USDC only.

How to verify any of this

1. Open a market in the pm-AMM reference app or on Solscan (links on every card) — the question, end time and state are on-chain. 2. Re-fetch the outcome source named in the criteria at the resolution time and compare it with the fetched value in the proof. 3. Follow the create / swap / resolve transaction links — every claim on this page traces to a transaction or a saved source response.

Resolution on pm-AMM is manual and centralized by design — the market creator resolves. Sentinel makes that authority auditable instead of hiding it: criteria are fixed before the market opens, the outcome source is public, the raw source response is saved before the on-chain resolution call, and this page keeps the losses.

The agent loop creates markets but never trades them (conflict of interest). The one-command demo (npm run demo) buys YES once to prove the trade path end to end — those buys are the ones shown on the demo markets above.
